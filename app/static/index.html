Event contracts, in brief

01 / A question

Yes or No

Each contract represents an outcome defined by its market rules. Check the exact question and resolution source before interpreting a market.

02 / A price

Market-implied signal

A contract price from $0.01 to $0.99 may be read as a rough market-implied probability. It is a trading price, not a guarantee or objective forecast.

03 / A resolution

Settlement

Contracts settle according to their published rules and source. A winning contract generally pays $1; a losing one pays $0. Fees and execution prices affect results.

04 / A risk

Loss is possible

For a long position, the full amount paid can be lost. Liquidity, spread, fees, stale prices, and ambiguity in resolution rules matter.
